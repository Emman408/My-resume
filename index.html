About

Two disciplines, one operator.

I am Emmanuel A. Ajilore, currently in technical support and operations at FluxHill Technologies, based in Saint John, NB. My day-to-day is helping users solve real problems on real systems: endpoint issues, network troubleshooting, account access, the things that keep teams moving.

Before IT became the focus, I spent years in graphic design, video production, and brand identity work. That history did not disappear when I added network admin and cybersecurity to my training. It became the way I think about clarity, communication, and what an interface should actually do.

If you need someone who can resolve a ticket, document the fix cleanly, and also redesign the internal portal it lives on, that is the shape of what I bring.
